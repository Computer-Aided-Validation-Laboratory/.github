# Computer-Aided Validation Laboratory (CAVL)

**Simulation-driven experimental design | Measurement uncertainty | Model validation**

The Computer-Aided Validation Laboratory (CAVL) is a research laboratory developing open-source software for simulation-driven experimental design, measurement analysis, uncertainty quantification and engineering model validation.

Our goal is to help researchers and engineers **design better experiments, understand measurement uncertainty and make evidence-based engineering decisions** by connecting physics simulations with experimental measurements.

Our research and software development are led by a team at the UK Atomic Energy Authority (UKAEA), working with academic and industrial collaborators.

## Our Software

### PyVale — Your Virtual Engineering Laboratory

<a href="https://github.com/Computer-Aided-Validation-Laboratory/pyvale">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Computer-Aided-Validation-Laboratory/pyvale/main/images/pyvale_logo.png" alt="PyVale logo" width="420">
</a>

**PyVale** is our flagship Python platform for simulation-driven experimental design. It connects physics simulations, sensor simulation, measurement inference and information exploitation to support the design and analysis of engineering experiments.

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Computer-Aided-Validation-Laboratory/pyvale) | [Documentation & Examples](https://computer-aided-validation-laboratory.github.io/pyvale/) | [Python Package](https://pypi.org/project/pyvale/)

### Riley — Scientific Camera Simulation

<a href="https://github.com/Computer-Aided-Validation-Laboratory/riley-raster">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Computer-Aided-Validation-Laboratory/riley-raster/main/images/riley_logo.png" alt="Riley logo" width="420">
</a>

**Riley** is a high-performance software rasteriser written in Zig for synthesising realistic images from finite element simulations. It supports scientific camera simulation and digital image correlation uncertainty quantification, and is integrated into PyVale's rendering framework.

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Computer-Aided-Validation-Laboratory/riley-raster) | [Python Package](https://pypi.org/project/riley-raster/)
